# Audit 2: cubby (level L2) at db85fec (2026-09-28), release v0.2.0

Standards: `CODING-RULES.md` and `SECURITY-CHECKLIST.md`, version 2026-09-25.1.
Method: `/audit-code` procedure, scorecard sub-criteria, a fresh clone of `main` at db85fec
(the v0.2.0 tag), followed as a new developer would (`make install && make check`).
Previous: [audit 1](2026-09-28-audit-1.md), 5.7/10 with the security gate BLOCKED (7 FAIL, 5 NOT_VERIFIED).

Cubby went through six reviewed pull requests since audit 1. Every defect audit 1 measured is
fixed with a named reproducer. The independent reviews and the new tests found eight more
defects, all now fixed:
- non-UTF-8 and line-separator names that could not be undone;
- an agent that died on SIGTERM;
- a signal-handler deadlock;
- a silently ignored misspelt config key;
- pause files that crashed the agent;
- `explain` misreading symlinks;
- `init` shadowing a config.

The remaining distance to 9 is operational, not correctness:
- `cli.py` is 730 lines;
- the logs are not correlated to runs;
- no percentile baseline or 10x limit has been measured;
- alert delivery has not been observed;
- the runbook has not been executed by anyone but its author;
- there is one maintainer.

## Release blockers (applicable P0 failed or not verified)

NOT_VERIFIED:
1. **SEC-14-003** (MFA on every account with access): one account; the CI token has no `user`
   scope, so the 2FA flag cannot be read (`gh auth status`: scopes gist, read:org, repo,
   workflow). The maintainer must confirm it.

FAIL: none found, within the scope checked below.

## Remediation plan (impact / effort)

1. [1h, high] Split `cli.py` into a package (commands, rendering, service wiring) under the
   400-line trigger. Lower `cmd_status` and `_find_dates` below C901 10, and enforce C901 in
   ruff. Raises MNT-02 from 6 to 9.
2. [30m, high] Add an AST test that fails on any `subprocess.run` or `Popen` call in `src/`
   without `timeout=`. Raises REL-01 from 7 to 9.
3. [1h, high] Put the run id and the cubby version on every log line of a pass, and the version
   on each ledger record. Raises OBS-01 from 7 to 9 and OBS-05 from 7 to 8.
4. [1h, med] Document the single-writer constraint and its limit in `docs/architecture.md` (SCL-01 7 -> 9). Benchmark with repetitions and percentiles at 1k, 10k, 20k and 200k files (10x),
   recording machine and method in `docs/PERFORMANCE.md`, with the breaking point and the next
   step. Raises PRF-01 from 7 to 9 and SCL-02 from 6 to 9.
5. [30m, med] Have an independent agent execute the runbook in a throwaway HOME (pause,
   evidence, history, undo, rollback, removal), with the service manager faked, and record it.
   Raises RCV-06 from 7 to 8.
6. [user, 1 min] The maintainer confirms 2FA (SEC-14-003) and runs `cubby doctor --notify` on
   the Mac. This unblocks the gate and moves OBS-03 from 7 to 9.
7. [30m, low] Factor the shared test fakes into `tests/helpers.py`. Raises TST-10 from 8 to 9.
